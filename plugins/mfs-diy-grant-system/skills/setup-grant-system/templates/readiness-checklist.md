# AI Grant System Readiness Checklist

Your AI grant system can only be as good as what you feed it. Score each foundation, then use your total to decide where to start.

**Scoring:** 2 = Ready, 1 = Partly there, 0 = Not yet

## The six foundations

### 1. Strategic plan
- **Ready (2):** A board-adopted plan from the last three years, with goals for programs and funding.
- **Partly (1):** An older plan, a plan that was never adopted, or goals that live only in board minutes.
- **Not yet (0):** No written plan.
- **Why it matters for AI:** Claude uses your plan to connect each grant to where your organization is headed. Without it, proposals read as a list of activities.

### 2. Theory of change
- **Ready (2):** A written theory of change or logic model that names your programs and the short-term, intermediate, and long-term outcomes for each.
- **Partly (1):** You know your outcomes, but they are not written down in one place, or they mix outputs (counts) with outcomes (changes for people).
- **Not yet (0):** No written outcomes.
- **Why it matters for AI:** Funders want outcomes. Claude will not make them up, so they need to come from you.

### 3. Clean financials
- **Ready (2):** A board-approved budget for this year, books reconciled monthly, your most recent 990 filed, an audit or financial review if your size calls for one, and expenses you can split by program.
- **Partly (1):** Most of these are in place, but program costs are hard to separate or the books run behind.
- **Not yet (0):** No current approved budget, or the books are more than three months behind.
- **Why it matters for AI:** Grant budgets must match your real numbers. Claude builds budgets only from figures you provide.

### 4. Program data and stories
- **Ready (2):** For each program, you track outputs and at least one outcome every quarter, the numbers are less than a year old, and you keep a folder of stories and quotes shared with consent.
- **Partly (1):** You track counts but not outcomes, the data is scattered, or stories are collected without written consent.
- **Not yet (0):** Little or no regular program data.
- **Why it matters for AI:** Every number in a grant needs a source. Claude uses placeholders when data is missing, so missing data means more blanks to fill.

### 5. Master grant
- **Ready (2):** One current document with your organization overview, program descriptions, a needs statement with sourced statistics, outcomes by program, approved language, and current funders, updated in the last six months.
- **Partly (1):** You reuse a past proposal as your starting point, or your master grant is more than a year old.
- **Not yet (0):** Each grant starts from scratch.
- **Why it matters for AI:** This is the single source Claude drafts every proposal from. It is the biggest time saver in the whole system.

### 6. AI policy
- **Ready (2):** An adopted policy that says what information can and cannot go into AI, which tools are approved, and that a person reviews everything before it goes out.
- **Partly (1):** Informal rules, or a draft that was never adopted.
- **Not yet (0):** No policy.
- **Why it matters for AI:** Your policy sets the rules Claude follows. It protects the people you serve, your donors, and your reputation with funders.

- **0 to 5: Foundations first.** Your AI policy and theory of change come first. The grant system will work much better once they are in place.

## Technology basics (not scored)

- [ ] A paid Claude plan (needed to install plugins)
- [ ] Shared file storage for grant files (Google Drive or Microsoft 365)
- [ ] One person named as the main grant system user
- [ ] A password manager and two-factor sign-in on key accounts

## Suggested order to build

1. **AI policy** first, so the rules are set before any information goes into AI.
2. **Theory of change**, so your outcomes are clear.
3. **Master grant**, built from your theory of change and program data.
4. **Goal budget** (optional), to show donors and funders what full funding would make possible.

Your strategic plan and financials come from your board and your finance team or accountant. Claude can help you organize what you have, but it cannot replace those.
