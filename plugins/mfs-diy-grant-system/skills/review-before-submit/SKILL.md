# Review Before Submitting

Give the organization a clear picture of whether a grant is ready to submit, and what to fix if it is not.

Apply organization-profile and grant-guardrails throughout.

## Inputs

- The current draft
- The funder's questions, limits, and guidelines
- The project budget, if any
- The list of required attachments (from start-grant, or the guidelines)

Always ask for the funder's questions if they were not shared. A review without them can only check writing, not completeness.

## Step 1: Completeness

For each funder question:

| Question | Answered? | Within limit? | Notes |

- Flag any question not answered, or answered only in part.
- Flag answers over the limit, with the current count.
- Flag any funder priority from the guidelines the draft does not address.

## Step 2: Accuracy and consistency

- Every placeholder, `[CONFIRM]`, or `[XX]` still in the draft
- Every outside statistic: has a source and year in the sentence, appears in Sources Cited, and is no more than three years old
- Organization numbers match the Master Grant Narrative and Program Data, or the user has confirmed a newer figure
- The request amount is the same everywhere: summary, narrative, budget, and cover form
- Program names and terminology match organization-profile
- Outputs are not described as outcomes
- Stories and quotes are from the consented folder
- No other organization's name or program appears (common when reusing an old proposal)

## Step 3: Attachments

Check each required attachment against the grant's folder:

| Attachment | In folder? | Current? | Notes |

Flag a 990 or audit that is not the most recent, a board list that is out of date, and missing letters of support.

## Step 4: Writing

Only after the checks above, note up to five improvements that would make the biggest difference: a weak opening, a buried request, an unclear outcome, or a section that does not match the funder's priorities. Keep the organization's voice.

## Step 5: Verdict

Give one of:

- **Ready to submit** after the approver signs off
- **Ready after small fixes:** list them
- **Not ready:** list what is blocking

Remind the user who approves a grant before submission (from organization-profile). Offer to note the verdict on the grant's row on the Grant Calendar, only after they say yes.

Never submit the grant. The organization submits it.

When the user says it was submitted, hand off to award-and-stewardship.
