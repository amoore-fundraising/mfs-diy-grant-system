## About this document

Claude reads this document before every grant task: finding funders, drafting proposals and budgets, reviewing before you submit, managing awards, writing reports, and updating your master grant. It tells Claude who you are, how you talk about your work, what your AI policy allows, and where your files live.

If something here is out of date, Claude will be out of date too. Update this document when your programs, staff, approvers, or links change. If a field is left blank, Claude will treat it as unknown and ask you instead of guessing.

Keep "Grant System Profile" in the title so Claude can find it. Save it in your file storage, or add it to your Claude Project's files.
